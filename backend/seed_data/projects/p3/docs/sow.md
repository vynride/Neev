# Statement of Work: Pulse PM

**Between:** Barabari Collective (the team) and Northgate Events (the client).
**Project fee:** INR 70,000, fixed price. **Period:** 10 August 2026 to 28 September 2026.

## Work covered
An internal project-management tool as described in the client brief. Access model is a single admin who manages staff accounts. All staff have the same access to projects and tasks.

## Revision rounds
- Two rounds of revisions in total, one after the 28 August demo and one after staging on 21 September.
- Each round stays open for 3 working days because the timeline is short.
- New features, new user roles or new screens are change requests.

## Payment milestones
1. 40% (INR 28,000) on signing.
2. 30% (INR 21,000) on acceptance of the boards and dashboard demo.
3. 30% (INR 21,000) on go-live and handover.

Invoices are due within 7 days.

## Change-request process
1. Requests made on calls or WhatsApp are written up by the team and sent back to the client to confirm.
2. The team replies within 2 working days with effort, cost and the effect on the 28 September date.
3. The client chooses one option in writing: add it now with a new date and cost, swap it for an existing item of similar size, or move it to a phase two after go-live.
4. No work starts on a change before Sameer Bhatia approves in writing.

## Client responsibilities
- Email provider account and API key by 2 September 2026.
- Feedback within 2 working days.
- Each day of delay in access or feedback may move the delivery date by one day, confirmed in writing.

## Support after launch
21 days of bug fixing after go-live. Hosting and email provider charges are paid by the client.
