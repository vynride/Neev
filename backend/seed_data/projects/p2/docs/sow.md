# Statement of Work: SkillBridge LMS

**Between:** Barabari Collective (the team) and SkillBridge Coaching Centre (the client).
**Project fee:** INR 1,10,000, fixed price. **Period:** 29 June 2026 to 30 September 2026.

## Work covered
An online learning platform as described in the client brief: course catalogue, enrolment and player, educator area, Stripe payments, Clerk login, deployment on the client's domain and a handover document.

## Revision rounds
- Two rounds of revisions on student-facing pages.
- One round on the educator area.
- Each round closes 5 working days after a demo.
- New features or new pages are change requests.
- Performance complaints need an example: which page, which device, what happened. The team fixes issues it can reproduce as bugs, at no charge.

## Payment milestones
1. 25% (INR 27,500) on signing.
2. 25% (INR 27,500) after the course browsing and player demo.
3. 30% (INR 33,000) after payments and educator area are accepted.
4. 20% (INR 22,000) when the site is live on the client's domain.

Invoices are due within 7 days.

## Change-request process
1. The team records the request in writing.
2. Within 3 working days the team shares effort, cost and the effect on the timeline.
3. Work starts only after written approval from Devendra Kulkarni by email or WhatsApp.

## Client responsibilities
Course videos and thumbnails for 8 courses by 14 August 2026. Access to domain DNS settings at least 7 days before go-live. The client remains the owner of the domain, the Stripe account and the business email. The team will not change email settings.

## Support after launch
30 days of bug fixing. Running costs of Vercel, Clerk, MongoDB and Stripe fees are paid by the client.
