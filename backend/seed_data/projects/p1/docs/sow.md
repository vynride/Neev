# Statement of Work: Threadline Store

**Between:** Barabari Collective (the team) and Threadline Handlooms (the client).
**Project fee:** INR 85,000, fixed price. **Period:** 20 July 2026 to 10 October 2026.

## Work covered
An online store as described in the client brief: storefront, cart, Stripe Checkout, coupons, customer accounts, admin product management and an analytics tab, plus deployment and a handover document.

## Revision rounds
- Two rounds of design revisions on the storefront look and feel, each within 5 working days of a demo.
- One round of revisions on the admin area.
- Feedback must come as one list. Feedback after a round closes is a change request.
- Bug fixes do not count as revisions.

## Payment milestones
1. 30% (INR 25,500) on signing.
2. 40% (INR 34,000) on acceptance of the checkout and admin demo, planned for 11 September 2026.
3. 30% (INR 25,500) on launch and handover.

Invoices are due within 7 days.

## Change-request process
1. Any request outside the agreed scope is written down by the team as a change request with a short description.
2. The team replies within 3 working days with the effort in days, the cost and the effect on the deadline.
3. Work on the change starts only after the client approves in writing. A WhatsApp message from Rohini Malhotra is acceptable.
4. Small changes under 2 hours may be absorbed at the mentor's discretion, up to three times.

## Client responsibilities
Product photos, descriptions and prices by 28 August 2026. Replies to questions within 2 working days. Delays in content or access move the deadline by the same number of days.

## Support after launch
30 days of bug fixing after launch. New features are quoted separately.
